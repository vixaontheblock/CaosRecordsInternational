# CAOS RECORDS V2 — design notes

This pass intentionally moves the project away from the sparse black-grid label template.

## Direction

- Editorial rather than empty-minimal.
- Black / warm paper / charcoal rhythm instead of one endless black background.
- The official globe logo is treated as a major visual asset: oversized, cropped and repeated at deliberate moments.
- Content density comes from story, typography, structure and real company information — not fake artists or fake milestones.

## Removed

- Meridian/checkerboard background motif.
- Placeholder corporate email addresses.
- Fake TBA releases.
- Placeholder artist cards that looked unfinished.
- Fake form success behavior.
- Empty “currently” feature box.

## Added

- Stronger hero using the official white logo.
- Brand statement and manifesto.
- Service / relationship model.
- CAOS philosophy and working approach.
- Panama origin section.
- Current-stage section.
- Full company story.
- Expanded founders presentation.
- Instagram feature.
- Richer footer and contact routes.
- Conceptual Records page until real releases exist.
- Honest roster state until artist names/assets are approved.
- Booking form that opens a real email draft to `info.caosrecords@gmail.com`.

## Still needed from CAOS

- Confirmed artist roster.
- Approved artist photos, bios, genres and social/music links.
- Any real first-release information when public.
- Final domain confirmation before launch.
- Later: custom animated logo intro and motion system.
